Success behaviours

5 behaviours assessed for every person in the framework.

  • Ownership & Execution Discipline

    Demonstrates commitment to goals, delivers outcomes reliably, and follows through with accuracy and consistency.

  • Problem Solving & Critical Thinking

    Approaches challenges with structured thinking, identifies root causes, and applies solutions that lead to lasting improvements.

  • Collaboration & Influencing

    Builds strong relationships, communicates clearly, and works across functions to achieve shared goals and positive outcomes.

  • Detail Orientation & Quality Focus

    Consistently delivers accurate, complete, and high-quality output by focusing on details, standards, and continuous improvement.

  • Learning Agility & Coaching Mindset

    Demonstrates curiosity, adapts quickly to change, and develops self and others to stay ahead of evolving business needs.
